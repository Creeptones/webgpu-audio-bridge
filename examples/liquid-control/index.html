webgpu-audio-bridge — Liquid Neural Control

A small Liquid Time-Constant network (an LNN — continuous-time recurrent, input-modulated time constants, no tokens) runs on the CPU in a Worker at ~100 Hz and streams its 6-value control vector into the audio thread through BridgeWebNNSource.pushFromTypedArray() — the bridge's neural-model → AudioWorklet adapter, on its CPU path (no WebNN runtime). A tiny synth voice on the audio thread pullLatest()'s the newest vector each quantum and maps it to pitch, cutoff, amplitude, vibrato, detune, and tone. The net is the brain; the bridge is the nervous system. The network here is an untrained random liquid reservoir — the spike asks whether an LNN's continuous dynamics make interesting motion and whether the integration path holds; training a musical objective is the next layer.
