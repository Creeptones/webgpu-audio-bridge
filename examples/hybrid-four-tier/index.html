webgpu-audio-bridge — four-tier hybrid stack demo

The full hybrid stack composed in one demo. Four tiers, each owning a different latency class — the CPU carrier owns the latency-critical action; the GPU adds residual/timbre/space; a predictive macro layer forward-compensates the smooth controls:

What you're listening for. Move the freq slider — pitch follows instantly (fundamental on the CPU, tier 1+2). The cutoff sweep and auto-pan are tier-4 SMOOTH MACROS, pulled with pullPredictedLatest and led forward by the measured GPU readback wall. Toggle "Predict (negative latency)" off and on: with predict off, the cutoff/pan lag the gesture by the readback latency; with it on, they snap forward onto the gesture even though the GPU residual body is still ~85 ms behind. Press "Simulate GPU stall" — the harmonics fade for ~250 ms while the (centered) fundamental keeps playing; the carrier survives, no click.

What this is showing

Three of the four tiers already shipped as primitives; this demo is their composition. The one genuinely new wire is tier 4: a control-rate Bridge<S> of smooth macro fields carried as order-2 trajectories (position + velocity), pulled each quantum with pullPredictedLatest so each macro is rendered where it will be once the block is heard. The lead is the live lastReadbackMedianMs(), fed from the worker's measured GPU readback. Predict smooth macros, never the carrier — pitch/attack/note-on are discontinuous and stay on tiers 1+2; forward-extrapolating a step would pre-echo it. Safe by construction: a cold or jittery clock collapses tier 4 to a plain hold, leaving the carrier untouched.
