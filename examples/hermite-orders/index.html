webgpu-audio-bridge — Hermite-order A/B

A synthetic Worker producer stamps an order-4 control trajectory (an FM carrier's instantaneous frequency + its velocity, acceleration, and jerk) into a Bridge<S> at a deliberately low control rate. The AudioWorklet reconstructs that frequency between control frames and integrates it into a tone — and you choose how many derivatives the reconstruction matches at the seam:

  • cubic · C¹ — matches position + velocity (the 0.7.3 path).
  • quintic · C² — also matches acceleration (0.9.80). No curvature step.
  • septic · C³ — also matches jerk (0.9.81). No third-derivative step.

Drop the control rate toward 24–30 Hz and toggle the orders: the cubic seam buzzes (its second/third-derivative steps spray energy into the high sidebands); septic stays clean. Watch the spectrum's high skirt thin out as you climb orders — that's the f⁻³ → f⁻⁴ → f⁻⁵ rolloff the 0.9.85 FFT pin measures.

Note: this demo reconstructs the completed segment behind the newest frame (≈1.35 control periods of deliberate interpolation latency) rather than calling pullHermiteLatest — the convenience path is a freshest-interpolation primitive that pins t to the segment boundary, where all orders agree.
