webgpu-audio-bridge — audio-rate (block-mode) demo

A DedicatedWorker runs a WebGPU compute shader (or a CPU fallback) that synthesizes 1024 PCM samples per tick straight into a storage buffer. BridgeBlockProducer reads the buffer back, copies the samples into the bridge's f32Array slot, and pushes the block through the ring.

The AudioWorklet uses BridgeBlockConsumer to carve 128-sample quanta out of successive 1024-sample blocks. Each block lasts ~21 ms at 48 kHz, so one producer tick feeds 8 audio quanta. The producer paces at 50 Hz to stay just ahead of the consumer's ≈46.875 Hz consumption.

Honest latency floor: ring depth 4 × 1024-sample block ÷ 48 kHz = 85 ms input-to-audible. This is the floor — not a target. See the README's "Audio-rate mode" section for the math.
