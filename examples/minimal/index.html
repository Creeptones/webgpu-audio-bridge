webgpu-audio-bridge — minimal demo

A DedicatedWorker runs a GPU compute pass (or a CPU fallback when WebGPU isn't available) at ~60Hz, publishes the result into a SharedArrayBuffer via the bridge, and an AudioWorklet reads the freshest frame each render quantum and synthesizes audio.

The whole point: nothing in AudioWorkletProcessor.process() blocks or waits on the GPU.
