webgpu-audio-bridge — fast-lane (input lane) demo

Two bridges share the AudioWorklet. The macro bridge publishes a slow filter / detune envelope at 60 Hz (no GPU here, but the same path). The input bridge carries discrete user events — keyboard, mouse, MIDI — via a dedicated Bridge<InputSchema> that the worklet drains every quantum with BridgeInputLane.pullAll(...).

Click anywhere or tap a key (computer keyboard or MIDI). Input lands in the synth within ~3–6 ms on tuned hardware; the slow macro envelope coevolves in the background.
