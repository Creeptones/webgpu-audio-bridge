webgpu-audio-bridge — hybrid residual bench

Programmatic measurement of the underflow-tolerance improvement of BridgeBlockConsumer.processAdd() over the replace-mode process(). Drives a controlled GPU-stall sequence against the same producer, switches the worklet between Replace and Hybrid modes back-to-back, and reports the output-RMS during a programmed stall window in each. Replace-mode RMS drops near zero (zero-fill click); Hybrid-mode RMS stays at the CPU-carrier floor (audible continuity).

Per-quantum hot-path cost is measured in npm run bench via the 0.9.41 cell in bench/Bridge.bench.ts. The headline number is reproduced at the bottom of this page for reference; the dynamic measurement below covers the semantic property the Node bench can't see.
