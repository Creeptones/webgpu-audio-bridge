Notes on the run
================

Median delta of 20 ns is at or below the bench's noise floor. Both
code paths' p99/p999/max are noisier than the per-pull median signal
because SpiderMonkey has a fatter GC tail than V8 (mean is 200-400 ns
above median on Firefox vs ~30 ns above on Chromium/Node V8).

The NEGATIVE p99 and max deltas (notify being apparently FASTER than
noNotify in the tail) are not real signal. They're an artifact of the
bench measuring the two paths sequentially: whichever path happens to
catch a GC pause or a Windows scheduling burp during its 1000 batches
shows a fatter tail. With a ~20 ns signal and a ~5 μs tail variance,
the tail can't see the signal. The median is the only number that
isolates the actual per-pull notify cost.

So the read for SpiderMonkey is the same as V8: ~20 ns delta at the
median, indistinguishable from zero at the resolution of either V8 or
SpiderMonkey's clock + bench harness. SpiderMonkey is doing the same
empty-wait-list short-circuit V8 does, just with more GC noise on top.
