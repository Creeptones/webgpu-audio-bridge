webgpu-audio-bridge — end-to-end latency bench

Measures the time between the producer's performance.now() at push and the audio thread's currentTime when the frame is consumed by pullLatest(). Reports median / p50 / p95 / p99 / max over a sliding window.

The headline number for a real audio pipeline is p99 under load — that's what controls glitching. Idle numbers are reassuring but unrepresentative.

Notes for CI

The Playwright spec at tests/browser/latency.spec.ts runs a fixed-duration version of this bench (CPU stub, no audio output) and asserts the p99 stays below a budget. The page itself is for interactive measurement, especially across browsers and hardware where headless CI doesn't help.
